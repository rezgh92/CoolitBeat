## 7. Manifold flow-uniformity criterion
Level-2 channel entry velocity 1.16 m/s, Re 1718; pressure variation along it ~401 Pa (friction 401 Pa vs momentum 337 Pa, opposite sign) against a branch (core) dP of 1362 Pa -> ratio 0.29. Rule of thumb: ratio <= 0.1-0.2 for <10 % flow non-uniformity.
