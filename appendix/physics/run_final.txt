## 2b. Why the coupled mean is higher: plate NTU and the isothermal-wall caloric term

Lateral conduction makes the HMMC surface near-isothermal at the cell scale, so the fluid rises exponentially (front-loaded heat pickup), not linearly.  Mean fin-side wall rise: uniform-flux/linear = dT_out(1/2 + 1/NTU) [1-D model]; isothermal = dT_out/(1-exp(-NTU)).

| design | h_eff kW/m2K | NTU | wall rise linear K | wall rise isothermal K | effective caloric fraction | case dT (1-D, linear) K | case dT (isothermal) K | penalty K |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| HMMC 0.10/0.10 x 1.0 mm (base 1.0 mm) | 158 | 1.56 | 14.45 | 16.03 | 0.625 | 24.15 | 25.73 | +1.58 |
| HMMC-fine 0.075/0.075 x 1.0 mm (base 1.0 mm) | 239 | 2.36 | 11.70 | 13.98 | 0.680 | 21.39 | 23.68 | +2.28 |
| HMMC-ultrafine 0.05/0.05 x 0.6 mm (base 1.0 mm) | 340 | 3.36 | 10.10 | 13.12 | 0.738 | 19.80 | 22.81 | +3.02 |
| CoolIT-like 0.2/0.2 x 4 mm split-flow (base 2.0 mm) | 102 | 1.00 | 18.93 | 19.97 | 0.582 | 31.91 | 32.96 | +1.04 |

(The split-flow plate is NOT isothermal over 54 mm, so the linear budget applies to it; the HMMC is isothermal over its 2 mm cells, so the isothermal budget applies - confirmed by the coupled 2-D runs in sections 2 and 6.)
