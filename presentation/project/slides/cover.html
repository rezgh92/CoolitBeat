PRISM-15 Rev A

A single-phase copper cold plate designed to beat CoolIT’s 15 kW plate at CoolIT’s own operating point, in CoolIT’s own units.

Architecture, dimensioned design, parametric CAD, predicted datasheet, manufacturing route and programme. Every performance number is a model prediction: no hardware exists yet.

Benchmark: CoolIT Systems, Tech Brief: 15 kW Coldplate (June 2026) · Arbiter: OCP Single-Phase Direct-to-Chip Cold Plate Test Specification v1.0.0

This deck is the design review for PRISM-15 Rev A. The brief was to set CoolIT’s 15 kW cold plate as the benchmark and design a conventionally manufacturable plate that beats it. Everything shown is a model prediction until the first coupons and plates are tested; the programme’s first three months exist to replace these predictions with measurements.
