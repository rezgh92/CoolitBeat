Layer 2 · Grate

Two laminations: the lands that tie, the web that meters

Land plate, 1.30 mm

A 1.2 mm window over every kerf; a 2.7 × 0.8 mm recess on the seven inlet lines so the jet lands on a 0.75 mm lip, never on a fin. Continuous lands brazed to the fin tips over at least 70 % of their length. Laser-scribed capillary-break grooves keep filler out of the windows.

Meter web, 0.40 mm

378 inlet orifices, 0.50 × 0.99–1.12 mm in five size steps, alternating ±1.0 mm off the line; 378 outlet-row holes that balance the two segments beside each line. 2.31 kPa at 1.54 m/s; discharge coefficient 0.72 assumed, measured on coupons in E2. Laser-pierced, electropolished, 100 % backlit vision before and after braze.

The web is the only part that changes between packages: a new zoning is a laser file and two to three weeks, not a die. Artwork files: artwork_prism15_ttv.dxf, artwork_prism_g_example.dxf.

The grate is two laminations because the recess must open toward the web and no single-plate face operation cuts that undercut cleanly. Putting the orifices 1.3 mm above the braze plane is what makes the meter immune to braze fillets, which is the usual failure of microjet plates.
