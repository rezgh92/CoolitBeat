Because every part is built from the one parameter file, a change decided at gate G1, such as the 121.0 versus 122.6 mm grate outline or the pin diameter, regenerates the parts, the assembly check, the drawings and the mass table in minutes. The core at full resolution takes about ten minutes; everything else seconds.
