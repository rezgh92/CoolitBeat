The recommended public claim (decision D1) excludes the 4 kW point, the low-flow average and the 100 cm² average, and is released only after gate G2 measurement.

A claim of “beats on every metric” would be false and would be shown false by the first independent lab. The scoped claim covers the maximum at every flow, the average at the published point, uniformity, capacity, inlet headroom and pressure drop, and says plainly that the plate costs more, ships with a filter and has no field record.
