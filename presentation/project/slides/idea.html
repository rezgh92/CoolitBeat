The mechanism

Every parcel of coolant travels 7.7 mm, not 54

1. Fifteen metered lines

A separate 0.4 mm web of 756 graded orifices feeds each line through a recess onto a grate lip. Flow is uniform within ±2.2 % at every flow, because meter and plenum both scale with velocity squared.

2. Short cells, fine fins

0.15 mm fins at 0.15 mm gaps, 2.0 mm tall, kerfed at each line. Each segment starts at inlet temperature; fins are sized for heat transfer and fouling, not for pressure drop.

3. Tied fin tips

Every fin tip is brazed to the grate. The 0.85 mm base sees pressure only between ties 0.3 mm apart, so it carries 1 MPa with zero bow and holds a pad or liquid metal at its rated resistance.

Section from the CAD assembly across the lines at y = 10 mm. Split-flow plates send coolant 54 mm along coarse 0.2 mm fins; the far edge runs on coolant already 12.7 K warmer.

The three rules do most of the work. The flow length is the lever on the caloric term, the fine fins on the convection term, and the ties on the base term and on the interface. The grate is two laminations so the recess can face the web and so braze fillets can never reach an orifice.
