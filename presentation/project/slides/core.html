Base term 2.79 K at 0.85 mm; 2.63 K at 0.80; 3.29 K at the 1.0 mm fallback. The thin base is gated on experiment E1 proving that one unbonded land strip can be seen at proof.

Skiving at 0.15 by 0.15 by 2.0 mm in copper is at the edge of current capability, which is why the first-article plan starts with a six-blank skiving trial and a Cpk estimate on fin thickness. The kerfs are cut with the field potted in wax so the fins do not deflect. The base is finished last, after the braze, so its thickness is set against the real tie plane.
