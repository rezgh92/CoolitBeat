Why length, not just flow

The two-dimensional cell model shows the average case temperature falling with cell length at every flux while the case ripple is flat between 5 and 8 mm. Grading length turned the architecture’s one conceded loss on uniform low-flux lids into parity.

GPU example shipped with the package

Four 26 × 33 mm dies at 350 W/cm² plus 16 HBM stacks on a 110 × 120 mm field: 17 lines, 5.5 mm over the dies and 11 mm over memory; the die block takes about 88 % of the flow and no memory stack sits downstream of a die.

Core, grate land plate, plenum and cap tooling are common across packages; only the 0.4 mm meter web and the pedestal cut change. Artwork tool: design/grate_artwork.py.

This is the commercial point of the metered grate. A split-flow plate needs a new skiving program and often a new manifold for every package; here a zoning change is an artwork file for a laser job shop with a two to three week turnaround.
