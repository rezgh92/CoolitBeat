81 % of the plate drop is inertial. The meter takes 2.3 kPa, the fins 1.1, the ports 1.6. Quick-disconnects dominate any tray loop, which is why we publish three boundaries.

CoolIT-like columns are our calibrated split-flow model, measured only at 18 LPM (34.4 / 44.2 K). External claim: at or below 10 kPa at 18 LPM and 30 kPa at 36 LPM with 20 mm ports.

Below 0.75 LPM per kW the 15 kW outlet exceeds 65 °C and the OCP sweep is run at reduced power; resistance is power-independent to first order. The pressure-drop test is the cheapest and first measurement: it exposes the meter’s discharge coefficient before any thermal test is run.
