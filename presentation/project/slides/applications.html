Why AI data centres first

The plate is sized and tested for the 15 kW class and scales to the GPU envelope on the same tooling. Buyers who control package, tray and loop can adopt the tray filter and the R+ grade.

SiC converters: a 27 % smaller junction swing

On a 1200 V SiC half-bridge with a sintered substrate the PRISM-DC core cuts the junction-to-coolant rise from 31.4 to 22.8 K per 400 W switch: 1.38× the loss per cm² of SiC, or four to five times the power-cycling life. Under a gap pad the advantage vanishes.

“SSD converters”

Read as SiC converters. Solid-state transformers and DC breakers fit; storage drives at 1 to 2 W/cm² gain nothing from this core.

Go-to-market: hyperscaler co-design through a non-Group-A contract manufacturer; NVIDIA through its thermal engineering team; OEM rack kits; transparency as marketing.

The A1 socket is contested: Group-A houses already ship liquid products for TPU and Trainium. The pitch to a hyperscaler is the zoned plate, the tray filter and the data pack together, supplied under our recipe and test cell at a contract manufacturer they already audit.
