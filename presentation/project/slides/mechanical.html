Mounting and integration

The clamp bears on the cap, never on the ears or the ties

Load frame and screw kits

The 1.5 mm annealed ears would see 47 to 195 MPa against a 40 MPa allowable, so an 85 g stainless frame bears on two flat cap lands over the plenum end frames. Eight dia-4 captive shoulder screws with preset springs: 480 N (SPR-60) or 880 N (SPR-110) per plate on a lidded package, 2,011 N on the test vehicle. Clamp repeatability 4 to 5 %.

Variants in the same tooling

HBM pedestal 0 to 0.40 mm cut in the same fly-cut for bare-die packages; a PRISM-G envelope of 127 × 137 mm with 17 zoned lines at 1.03 to 1.15 kg; four test vehicles from the 4 kW single-die point to 400 W/cm² and 30 kW.

SKU limit; budget 1,240

Mass is 67 g over the 1,240 g budget on the cap lane’s basis and 92 g under the 1,400 g limit with the frame; a 0.6 mm 316L separator (−106 g) recovers it. A thinner cap sheet is ruled out by draw thinning at the arch ends. Decision at G1.

Nothing in the clamp path touches the fin ties: the load goes from the cap lands down the plenum end frames into the solid rim. Pressure loads are self-equilibrated by the ties. That separation is what lets the same plate serve a 2 kN test clamp and a 480 N package clamp without a redesign.
